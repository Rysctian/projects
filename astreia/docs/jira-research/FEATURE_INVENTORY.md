None of those proposals claims a recovered Jira storage or concurrency model.

## Deliberate simplifications (P)

- One organization, no licensing or organization federation.
- Project-local configuration rather than company/team-managed modes or shared scheme inheritance.
- Stable native fields first; typed custom fields later; acceptance criteria are a proposed dedicated field, not claimed as universal Jira default.
- Immutable workflow versions with controlled migration; no arbitrary scripts or external rule plugins.
- Same-project hierarchy/links/boards initially; no cross-project move or ticket deletion. Archive and explicit cancelled resolutions preserve history.
- Structured search rather than JQL; simple ranking rather than a claim to reproduce Jira's ranking implementation.
- Portal intake first; email ingestion, integrations, full incident/change operations, asset inventories and complex SLA escalation are deferred.
